Apiary Statistics & Reports

Summary of your saved records. Select a year or view all years. For a selected year, colonies are counted if their registered dates overlap that year. All years shows the current active-colony count. Empty categories show zero, not estimated activity.

Losses, splits and replacements are event counts, not a calculated survival rate. Harvest totals use recorded pounds. Income and expense totals reflect recorded transactions; net is income minus expenses.

Apiary Profit & Loss Dashboard

Financial results from recorded income and expenses. Shared transactions remain unassigned to individual hives.

Net = income minus expenses, based on transaction dates and entered amounts. This is a cash-record summary, not a tax determination; it does not account for inventory, depreciation, or unpaid bills. Hive totals exclude shared/unassigned transactions.
