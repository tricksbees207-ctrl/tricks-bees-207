The Anatomy of a Worker Honey Bee

Get to know the remarkable structures that keep a worker honey bee flying, foraging, communicating, and caring for its colony. This dedicated education series will pair detailed weekly lessons with our original blueprint-style worker bee anatomy illustration.

The master diagram

One bee. Ten closer looks.

The worker bee blueprint anchors this series. We are preserving the approved master artwork and its ten numbered spaces so that each week builds on the same illustration rather than redrawing it.

Original artwork preserved
Worker Honey Bee Anatomy Blueprint

Our approved master is being prepared for its website edition. The original labeled diagram and empty Week 1–10 boxes will be displayed here, without altering the artwork.

The original master is not replaced by a simulated or newly generated bee diagram.

How each weekly lesson works

  1. Publish a full-page illustrated lesson covering one anatomy topic.
  2. Keep that exact, complete lesson page available for readers to open and study.
  3. After publication, place a scaled-down copy of the same complete page into its numbered box on the master blueprint.
  4. Keep earlier weeks accessible as the ten-box diagram fills in.

New lesson artwork and miniature pages are separate additions. They do not overwrite the approved bee illustration or the underlying editable layers.
